# Zhipu GLM

**Observed in older fiction:** GLM-4.5 sometimes used exposition, repetitive sentence frames, and reassuring epilogues. [Comparison](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lechmazur/writing_styles/main/inter_llm_comparison_summaries/kimi-k2-0905__vs__glm-4-5.txt). The December 2025 dataset also reports occasional language switches for GLM-4.5/4.6. [Dataset](https://github.com/lechmazur/writing_styles).

**Editing response, if present:** Remove repeated labels or a conclusion that restates the theme. Keep necessary explanation and a gentle voice when requested. For a requested single-language draft, correct an unintended switch only when its meaning is clear; preserve quotations, proper names, and intentional multilingual voice. Do not treat bilingual writing as a flaw. These observations do not establish a profile for later GLM releases.

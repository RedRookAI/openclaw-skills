# Moonshot Kimi

**Observed in older fiction:** Kimi K2-0905 sometimes stacked metaphors until events became unclear and produced incomplete final lines in the December 2025 comparative dataset. [Comparison](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lechmazur/writing_styles/main/inter_llm_comparison_summaries/kimi-k2-0905__vs__qwen3-max-preview.txt).

**Editing response, if present:** Retain the strongest useful image and clarify the literal action from information already supplied. If a sentence is incomplete, do not invent the missing outcome; ask for it when necessary or mark the gap. Preserve rich literary language when it works. Older observations do not establish these habits in newer Kimi versions, nor does a stronger aggregate benchmark result make every draft immune to them.

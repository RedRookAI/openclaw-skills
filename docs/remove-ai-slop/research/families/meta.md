# Meta Llama and Muse

**Observed in older fiction:** Llama 4 Maverick leaned toward internal reflection, sparse dialogue, and positive endings in the December 2025 dataset. [Source](https://github.com/lechmazur/writing_styles).

**Editing response, if present:** Cut reflection that restates an event without adding useful perspective. Preserve the author's chosen interior voice and point of view. Check that the ending does not declare a success absent from the facts. Do not add dialogue to a style edit merely because a dataset found little dialogue.

**Coverage limit:** This release does not infer a Llama-style profile for Muse or other Meta models. Use the general pass for them. Family branding is not evidence that models with different architectures and configurations share a writing habit.

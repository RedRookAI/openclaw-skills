# OpenAI GPT, o-series, and GPT-OSS

**Observed:** SlopBench (2026-09-27) reports flattened paragraph rhythm for GPT-5.4 Mini. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.33905v1#S6.SS3).

**Older fiction evidence:** GPT-5 at medium reasoning sometimes obscured events with dense metaphor and aphoristic labels in the December 2025 comparative dataset. [Comparison](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lechmazur/writing_styles/main/inter_llm_comparison_summaries/claude-opus-4-1-20250805-0K__vs__gpt-5-medium.txt). GPT-OSS-120B used blocky paragraphing and strongly positive endings. [Dataset](https://github.com/lechmazur/writing_styles).

**Editing response, if present:** Organize paragraphs around actual changes of topic. Remove ornamental interpretations that conceal the literal action. Retain useful imagery and voice. Check whether an upbeat conclusion is supported. Do not infer these tendencies for every GPT or o-series version; inspect other versions with the general pass.
